What is US Staffing?

US staffing agencies sit between two groups: companies that need workers, and people who want jobs. A recruiter's job is to find the right person for an open role — sourcing candidates, screening them for skills and availability, and guiding them through interviews all the way to a placement. Roles range from short-term contracts to permanent hires, across industries like IT, healthcare, engineering, and finance. It's a fast-moving job built almost entirely on phone calls, email, and judgment — and almost nobody gets a real training manual for it. That's what this site is.
